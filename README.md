# Alejandro Jiménez Calderón

Data Scientist · Economist · MSc in Artificial Intelligence

I develop analytical and AI systems for tax, customs and public-sector decision
support, combining economic analysis with machine learning. My work spans data
preparation and model evaluation, APIs and web interfaces, deployment
configuration and reproducible reporting.

I document assumptions and limitations and distinguish experimental results from
operational performance.

## Project experience

### Foreign trade and mining analytics — data platform

Contributed to ingestion and validation pipelines, PostgreSQL data layers, FastAPI
and web integration, and reproducible reports for extractive-sector analysis.
Automated tests and CI check data contracts and integration.

### Tax revenue forecasting — experimental research

Contributed to data preparation and reproducible comparisons of SARIMA,
machine-learning and neural forecasts, with chronological out-of-sample evaluation
and versioned predictions and metrics. No model has been promoted to operational
use.

### Tax risk and customs selectivity — suite in development

Contributed to graph and tensor models, rule-based assessment, APIs and web
integration for tax-risk and customs review prioritization. Experiment tracking,
orchestration, monitoring and deployment configuration are within the development
scope. Outputs support human review.

### Tax registry consistency — standalone analytical component

Contributed to versioned rules, latent and tensor analysis, data validation and
verifiable HTML reports. Tests and an end-to-end example use synthetic data.
Diagnostics identify registry inconsistencies; they do not estimate noncompliance
probabilities.
